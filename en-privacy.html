Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what the Fraimero iPhone app does with your data. Fraimero is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: your measurements and jobs stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. We collect a few anonymous usage statistics that never contain your measurements.

Your calculations and jobs

Everything you type and calculate, your calculator tape and memory, and your saved jobs are stored on your iPhone. Saved jobs and the count of free tries you have used are also kept in your own iCloud (Apple’s key-value storage, tied to your Apple ID) so they appear on your other devices; we cannot access it. The count of free tries is also kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. Result images and job PDFs are created on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them.

Motion sensors

Measure pitch reads your iPhone’s motion sensors while that screen is open to work out an angle. The readings are used on the device and are never stored or sent.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used. The App Store version of the app sends only these events: when it is installed, opened or closed; when onboarding is completed and the language and units chosen; which tool is opened; when a free try of a Pro tool is used and how many are left; when the purchase screen is shown and where from; when a purchase is started, completed or fails; restores and whether a purchase was found; when you share a result image or a job PDF; when you use Measure pitch; when you change the language; and when the App Store rating prompt is shown. The events never contain your measurements, results, job names or any text you type. They include the app version, device model, iOS version and language, and are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email or Apple ID. PostHog stores these events in the United States. It uses your IP address only to estimate an approximate location (country and city), then discards it; we configure it not to keep IP addresses. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is unlocked. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

Photos

If you choose “Save Image” when sharing a result, iOS asks for permission to add it to your Photos. The app never reads your photo library.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data from your device; saved jobs in your iCloud can be removed in the app before deleting it. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Because they are anonymous we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can.

Children

Fraimero is a tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
