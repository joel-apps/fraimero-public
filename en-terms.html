What Fraimero is

Fraimero is a calculator. It does arithmetic and geometry on the numbers you enter and shows the results, with drawings, to help you plan work. It is not engineering, architectural or legal advice, and it does not know your plans, materials, site conditions or local rules.

Building codes

Stair limits (maximum riser, minimum tread, headroom) start at common defaults from the International Residential Code (IRC) and can be changed. Fraimero never says that a result meets a code. Codes differ by city, county and state and change over time. You are responsible for knowing and following the code that applies to your work, and for inspections.

Check before you cut

Measurements you enter can be wrong, and real lumber, framing and sites are never perfect. Always check critical numbers (for example by measuring, or with a second method) before cutting, ordering materials or building. Quantities for materials are estimates; order extra for waste, cuts and breakage.
