Common questions

Is it a subscription?

No. The calculator, triangle and pitch, and concrete tools are free. Pro is a single purchase that unlocks stairs, rafters, walls, materials, miter and crown, saved jobs and PDFs, for good. Each Pro tool can be tried 3 times for free.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I type feet, inches and fractions?

Type it like the tape: 5 FT 7 ½ gives 5′ 7½″. For other fractions, type the inches, tap IN, then the fraction with the / key (7 IN 9/16).

How accurate are the results?

Lengths are computed with exact fractions and rounded only for display, to the precision you choose (1/2 to 1/64). Every tool is tested against published worked examples. Results are still estimates: check them and follow your local building code. See the Terms.

Why does the Spanish say “stringer” and “rafter”?

Labels use the words crews in the US actually say on the job, with the formal Spanish word underneath. If your crew says it differently, tell us.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Only purchases need a connection.
